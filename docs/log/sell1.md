**Where it stands today (the seed, 17 November).** 253 of the 291 active items are packaged (188 packaged food, 20 cleaning, 14 beauty, 10 water, 10 bakery with a real barcode, 6 baby, 3 pet, 2 meat with a real barcode); 38 are loose (25 produce, 11 meat, 2 bakery); **none is label-ready yet**. With the setting on today, every one of the 253 would read "coming soon". Hence the order of work: the label day, the readings, the approvals (LB1's tools), then turn it on.

**Turning it on (a person's decision; no code does it).** After the label day and the approvals, open the console's Readiness page: "packaged, not label-ready" must list nothing that is in stock (that is the gate A item). Then, in the database: `update settings set value = '{"enabled": true}' where key = 'sell_requires_label';` and back with `{"enabled": false}`. Everything follows at once: the website within a minute (its page cache), the chat and the catalogue feed on the next read. There is no console page for the setting yet.

**Decisions to know.**
- **A card that waits for its tap when the setting is switched on is refused at the tap**: `order_quote_issues` (0007's text, one condition added) sends it back to draft (`stale_quote`) like any other change, and the customer's rebuilt card leaves the item out (`basket_from_order` counts a line that is not ok as missing, so "same as last time", a reopened order and a stale card all name it as not available). **A confirmed or paid order is never recalled**: the gate stops a new quote, a new card and a confirmation, nothing already in the system.
- **Ingredients and nutrition** are "automatic" in the standard: a label reading the rules approved counts, when its source is the label. Allergens, hazard warnings and certificates need a person (the database already refuses the automatic party for them).
- **Search shows a coming-soon item, flagged**, so the chat can say so; it never returns one as orderable. The fused rank is kept within each group; orderable matches come first.
- A **restaurant dish** is never packaged; a restaurant stays orderable only through `approve_merchant()` (invariant 11), as before.
- The renamed originals (`find_products_unflagged`, `search_catalog_unflagged`, `web_catalog_product_unflagged`, `product_readiness_levels`) are internal: the website's role was **not** left a grant on the page read's original (it was revoked in 0226).
- **Not gated, on purpose:** a staff member acting for a customer or a rider (0107), and a picker who names a substitute by hand (the list the picker is offered is gated).
