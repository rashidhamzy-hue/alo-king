**Not the same as the reference, and not proven.**
- **Approve.** The reference's card has the product picture beside the name, a "Why" line written by the AI, and Reject, Approve and Skip in one row. Ours has no picture on a name card (the proposals table has none), the reason is the facts we hold (model-written Arabic, added words, Open Food Facts' name), and Reject sits under its own reason field because a reason is needed. The reference's tabs for dishes and prices: prices are a second-approval card here (a price change above the limit); dishes are Phase 8.
- **The "I looked at the label photo" tick and "a reason to reject" are required fields of the page, not rules in SQL** (the database keeps its optional note). A client that posts to the route directly is not asked.
- **Undo is for a name only.** Approving a picture replaces the earlier picture and nothing puts it back; a label reading, a payment, a refund and a setting are records. If a later session adds a function that reopens one, the toast takes one line (`flashRedirect`'s `undo`).
- **Intake.** The progress board's "read" step is judged from label-ready items, because the reading run (GitHub Actions) leaves no row the console reads; step 2 therefore shows done only once something is label-ready. The per-item review page (`/intake/:product`) has the tab bar and the new look from the stylesheet; its markup is the old one (its tests pin it).
- **Money.** The reference's "takings by method" is today's payments from `day_close_report`; it is the same figure the nightly message carries. There is no "safe count" tab: the count is on Day close, which is linked.
- **Settings.** The reference's Settings has People first and a village pause for ops leads; ours is admin only (the brief), keeps People at `/staff` and zones and villages at `/zones` (both linked), and puts the activity log of settings at its foot. "Alerts & app" is on More. The whole action log (`staff_actions`) is not on this page: its settings changes are in `config_changes`, with who and when.
- **Riders.** The reference's roster table has jobs a week, fees, tips and an on-time share, and a licence column; the brief says no rating, so the on-time share is not shown, and no jobs, fees or tips figures were asked for. The weekly shift grid and the applicants (from `/join/rider`) wait for RD1. "Written terms on file" is new data (0232): no rider has any on staging, so every rider reads "not on file" until a lead records them.
- **Two-person list is a decision** (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, UX2b 2): the approval limits, the second-person rule switched off, the cash caps and selling-requires-a-label switched on. A list longer or shorter is one SQL row each.
