**Not proven, and what to know.**
- **Nothing here is on staging yet**: 0236 to 0240 apply at the next deploy from `main`. No phone has run the pages; the camera input (`capture="environment"`) and printing from a phone were only seen in Chromium.
- **The minute the migrations land, every unit already on staging's shelf has no lot** (no real receipt exists there, so the backfill finds nothing): the picker reads "no lot: check the date on the pack" until goods come in through purchase orders. That is the honest state, not a fault.
- **The temperature alerts start with the first reading.** Until someone records one, nothing is alerted and the history says "log not started". After it, a missed reading alerts every day, including closed days (there is no closed-day rule; the hub is open daily).
- An expired lot at the **finish** of a pick (the staff line's "done", or a rider pickup when no pick queue is used) is booked and alerted, not refused (DECISIONS WH1a, 7); the console runner refuses it before that.
- No thermometer reading is read from the photo by a model (rule 12); the strategy's "photo read into a number" is a later, metered step if ever wanted.
- The Arabic of the new pages, and of the Receiving page (put into Arabic in this pull request at the coordinator's request of 18 November; DS2's components, its refusals in both languages), is model-written and unread (`docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md`, WH1a). Product names show in English where the catalog has no Arabic name.
- A short or damaged delivery is recorded, but the supplier's credit note is still made by hand (0025's page), as before.
- Migration numbers: 0236 to 0240 used; **0241 is next** (0219 and 0220 stay WEB3's).
