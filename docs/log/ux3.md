**Not the same as the reference, and not proven.**
- **The reference's example evening** (invented earnings) is not built: it would promise income. The fee range comes from the zones instead. The reference's "Free deliveries still pay" line is not said: no rule of ours states it. The reference's vendor flow (Accept, Busy or Decline buttons, a weekly statement) is not claimed: it is E1's and not built; the page says what we offer in words that are true today.
- **Shifts are a draft** (morning 08:00 to 15:30, evening 15:30 to 23:00, from the reference) in `settings.join`; so are the vehicles (motorbike, car, bicycle) and the vendor kinds. A person changes them in SQL (they are not on the Settings page).
- **A decision sends nothing and does not close the case**: the person answers from the Inbox case and resolves it there. A new rider is not told by code that they were accepted, and has no shift, no terms on file (the roster says so) and no weekly shift grid: RD1.
- **Vendor applications have no console page of their own.** They are Inbox cases (and, for a listed place, a claim on the Listings page); a Vendors page is E1's. `join_applicants_console('vendor')` is ready for it.
- **The licence answer decides nothing**: a person reads it. The village is free text matched to our list where it matches; the Arabic village names on an Arabic page come from `villages.name_ar`.
- **Never seen on a phone or on staging.** Screenshots are Chromium on this machine with invented data. 0233 and 0234 are applied only in the test database until the next staging deploy. **A real code has not been sent from a real WhatsApp to the customer number**; the reading of it is the existing, tested `claim_link_code`.
- Arabic, English and French wait for a reader (`docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md`, UX3). Bidi of an amount inside an Arabic sentence is isolated by code; look at it on a phone.
