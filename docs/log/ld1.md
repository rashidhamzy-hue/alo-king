**Decisions to know.** (a) Required fields always show; optional ones (caffeine, deposit, scent ...) only once approved, so a bottle of water does not say "gallon exchange: not confirmed yet". (b) Allergens, diet and ingredients show per approved fact as before; nutrition and claims need the whole item fact-complete (PD1's rule, and the same gate in the data). (c) Four seed items (AK-0190, 0204, 0205, 0206) have a price of 0 and no stock: they carry **no Offer** rather than a free price. (d) Salt has no schema.org property, so it is on the page only. (e) Source words changed from "declared, pack label" to "from the pack, checked <date>"; the old wording tests were updated for that, and `test/db/web-catalog-web1.test.ts` no longer expects `nutrition` inside `facts`.
